TRUTH CHECK Most cited facts hold up. That includes the earnings-call quotes, Microsoft's 12 GW and 1 GW a quarter, Oracle's 850 MW quarter, Deutsche Bank's $35B per GW and the $134.72 close. The weak point is the revenue math. The 90% rental share misreads Musk, who said on the Q3 call the goal is a roughly 50-50 split between internal use and renting.
